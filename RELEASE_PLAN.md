# Ashfall release plan

## Implementation brief

Build an original first-person Warlock-inspired arena with serious fire/thunder audio, readable magic combat, practice against bots, and separate multiplayer. Add ready checks and pre-fight countdown, persistent recoverable player profiles, weekly UTC community standings, and server-controlled top-three cosmetic rewards (skin choice and glowing name). Keep competitive damage and cooldowns equal. Verify actual browser interactions and server authority before publishing.

## Current release acceptance

- Real licensed flame and thunder recordings replace laser and cheerful cues. Preserve attribution and processing provenance.
- Spells have readable trails and impacts; lightning has a visible branching discharge, including remote casts.
- Host starts only when two or more players are ready. An eight-second countdown blocks combat and cancels on unready/disconnect.
- Profiles use opaque recovery codes, stored hashed on the server; recovery codes never enter URLs or logs. These are device profiles, not verified accounts.
- SQLite data persists outside public assets and Git. Weekly bounds are Monday 00:00 UTC.
- Only completed eligible authenticated community games score. No bot, short, abandoned, duplicate or repeatedly farmed same-day opponent results.
- Previous-week top three may choose a permanent cosmetic skin; weekly name glow expires. No paid or reward-derived combat advantage.
- Profile, leaderboard, rewards and readiness are usable from the browser; no fake seeded public winners.
- Tests cover persistence, UTC rollover, authorization, countdown cancellation, actual audio decoding, two browsers and practice regression.

### Game identity and required end state

Ashfall is an original first-person adaptation of the Warlock arena idea: aim and cast spells, build damage to increase knockback, push opponents off a shrinking rock arena into dangerous lava, and use mobility/defense to survive. Serious dark-fantasy atmosphere, readable combat, satisfying physical impacts and natural recorded fire/thunder rather than cartoon laser sounds. Preserve six spells: fireball, lightning, homing, meteor, blink and shield. WASD, mouse aiming, jump, spell controls, clear HUD/cooldowns, coherent victory/defeat feedback, understandable rules/tutorial/grimoire, settings, menus, pause behavior and return/replay paths must all work. Avoid unnecessary flashes, unreadably dark characters or excessive visual clutter.

1. Finish the practice experience: three competent bots, five-round tournament, working shop/upgrades/economy, round transitions, death/spectating where useful, tournament results, replay and menu return. Verify bot navigation, aiming, lava escape, blink, shield reflection and all spells. No stuck or unwinnable states. Keep pause genuinely pausing practice, while clearly explaining that online matches do not pause.
2. Finish multiplayer as a complete user journey: create/join/share six-letter rooms for 2-4 real players, recoverable connection errors, authoritative combat, synchronization, lobby readiness, gathering and eight-second preparation countdown. Host starts only with at least two connected players and everyone ready. Block movement/combat during countdown, cancel on unready or disconnect, safely reassign host, reset readiness for rematches. Provide useful dead-player spectating, results and rematch/leave paths. Close gaps between online and practice by implementing a coherent online tournament/shop flow if needed for the complete game; all economy and upgrades must be server-controlled and separate from cosmetics. Assess latency and improve interpolation/prediction/reconciliation where needed without weakening authority. Handle page blur, pointer-lock loss, reloads, reconnect and abandoned matches deliberately.
3. Finish persistent profiles: opaque random recovery code, SHA-256 hash stored server-side, local browser persistence with truthful warnings, create/name change/recover/import flows, copy/reveal/hide controls and robust errors. An empty or invalid import must not silently create or replace an account. Clear secret text on close/change; never put codes in URLs, logs, screenshots or public artifacts. Preserve identity across server restart. Device recovery proves token possession, not verified identity; do not advertise strong ranked authentication or Sybil resistance.
4. Finish weekly standings and rewards end to end: Monday 00:00 UTC week boundaries, current top-20, previous top-3, deterministic tie-breaking, understandable own standing and score eligibility. Score only server-completed eligible authenticated community matches; practice/bot games, short games, abandoned/disconnected outcomes, duplicates and repeatedly farmed daily pairs must not score. Current baseline is duration >=30s, winner 3 points and other participants 1, one unordered winner/opponent pair per UTC day, at most 20 scored games per account per week. Review multi-player anti-farm semantics and strengthen correctness; do not falsely claim collusion prevention. Previous-week top three choose one permanent premium-looking skin from Ember/Void/Storm, one choice per reward week, idempotent repeat claims, server-validated ownership/equip. Rewarded name color/glow expires with the weekly honor; collected skins remain. Both skin and glowing names must actually render on remote characters/in-game nameplates and relevant lobby/results/leaderboard UI, not only a CSS preview. No damage, cooldown, hitbox, movement or other combat buff from rewards. Test rollover and claim persistence on isolated fixtures, never seed fake winners in live data.
5. Finish audio: use natural recorded flame/torch/crackle for fireball, actual thunder for lightning, weighty appropriate meteor/impact/utility cues, restrained round/menu audio. Fireballs should sound like burning fire, not sci-fi pew-pew. Lightning should sound like thunder, not a cartoon zap. Current local licensed cues are available; preserve credits.json, cue-manifest.json, source/license provenance. Ensure casts/impacts from other players are audible once with sensible distance/panning, audio unlock works, mute truly silences, settings persist, rapid overlapping effects do not clip, and reset/leave cleans up sound. Do not claim subjective listening approval from decoding alone.
6. Finish visual quality: polished cohesive dark-fantasy environment, PBR rock/lava, lighting/atmosphere, recognizably distinct spell trails and impacts, branching lightning rather than a straight line, clear shields/blink/meteor/homing, visually distinct premium skins. Existing procedural wizard models are crude; improve their silhouettes/material/detail so the game feels intentional rather than a placeholder, while retaining readability and performance. Use licensed assets only. Provide meaningful low/high quality modes, sane draw calls/particles/lights, proper disposal and reduced-motion support.
